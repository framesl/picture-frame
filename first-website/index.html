Hello World!

Hello! I'm Sabrina Frame (she/her) and I'm a senior majoring in Graphic Design! In my free time I enjoy drawing, reading, listening to music, and playing video games (either story-based or with friends). I don't really interact with social media outside of watching movies, shows, and Youtube videos; therefore, I tend to approach media if it's inline with my interests, and then enjoy analyzing it psychologically.

The characters in any kind of media are most of the time what draws me to view it, so once I do interact with it, the main thing I lean towards is picking apart the characteristics of the people. Why they act the way they do, how they interact with others, and how their actions affect the world and those around them. Doing this also helps me to discuss the media with others, so if they've not really interacted with it and want to know whether or not they'd enjoy it, I'd be more equipped to tell them about it!

This is most likely impacted by the fact that I'm more drawn towards media where not everything is explicitly stated. A ton of the story-based games I play and books I read always have details hidden under the surface that you only really notice if you are looking carefully at what the characters do, so the easiest way for me to digest all of the details is for me to first understand the characters. This is also impacted by the fact that when I was younger I'd watch murder mystery shows with my grandma, so I tend to gravitate towards puzzles and mysteries that need to be solved (even if the mystery is characters in media). Overall, my media ecology demonstrates my personal interests, as well as what I'm drawn to when interacting with any kind of media.
